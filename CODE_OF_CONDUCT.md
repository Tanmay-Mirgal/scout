# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers of the **SCOUT** community, pledge to make participation in our project a respectful, welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We are committed to building a collaborative environment where contributors can share ideas, ask questions, provide feedback, and work together regardless of experience level or background.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive in discussions.
- Welcoming contributors of all experience levels.
- Giving and receiving feedback professionally.
- Focusing on ideas, code, and technical decisions rather than individuals.
- Helping new contributors learn and participate.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and approaches.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and working to correct them.
- Using inclusive and appropriate language.
- Prioritizing the health and sustainability of the SCOUT community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publicly sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly ignoring reasonable community or maintainer requests.
- Spam, malicious contributions, or intentionally harmful behavior.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional and collaborative open-source environment.

---

## Expected Collaboration

SCOUT is a collaborative project.

We encourage contributors to:

- Discuss major changes before implementing them.
- Keep Pull Requests focused and easy to review.
- Provide constructive feedback during code review.
- Respect project maintainers and other contributors.
- Ask questions when requirements are unclear.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment for first-time contributors.

Disagreements are a normal part of open-source development. When they occur, contributors should focus on technical reasoning, evidence, and the project's goals.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Project documentation
- Community communication channels
- Any public or private interaction representing the SCOUT project

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the SCOUT project in public spaces.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Reports of unacceptable behavior will be reviewed and handled appropriately.

Depending on the severity and context, maintainers may take actions including:

1. Providing a private or public warning.
2. Requesting changes to inappropriate behavior.
3. Temporarily restricting participation.
4. Rejecting or reverting contributions.
5. Removing a contributor from project spaces.
6. Permanently banning an individual from participation.

Maintainers will aim to apply these actions fairly and proportionally.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the project maintainers.

When reporting an issue, include relevant information where possible, such as:

- A description of what happened.
- Links to relevant discussions or Pull Requests.
- Screenshots or other supporting context, if appropriate.
- Any additional information that may help maintainers understand the situation.

Reports will be handled with reasonable confidentiality.

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are expected to:

- Apply the Code of Conduct consistently.
- Review reports fairly and respectfully.
- Avoid retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith.
- Clearly communicate moderation decisions when appropriate.
- Maintain a healthy and constructive contributor environment.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

When evaluating a potential violation, maintainers may consider:

- The severity of the behavior.
- Whether the behavior was intentional.
- Whether there is a repeated pattern.
- The impact on other contributors and the community.
- Whether the contributor is willing to correct their behavior.

The goal of enforcement is to protect the community and maintain a safe, respectful, and productive environment.
